# Digital Communication Book

The **Digital Communication Book** replaces traditional paper student diaries with a modern, transparent two-way messaging portal between homeroom teachers, subject teachers, and parents. It ensures that praises, behavioral observations, homework notes, and medical alerts are communicated and acknowledged in real time.

---

## 1. Key Communication Book Modules

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    Digital Communication Book                           │
├───────────────────┬───────────────────┬─────────────────────────────────┤
│  ⭐ Praise Cards   │  📝 Daily Notes   │  ⚠️ Behavioral & Dress Code     │
│  Teacher Kudos &  │  Homework tasks & │  Uniform infractions &          │
│  Milestone Badges │  materials needed │  conduct observations           │
└───────────────────┴───────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Entry Type | Icon | Purpose | Parent Action Required |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Praise & Commendation** | ⭐ | Celebrating student kindness, academic excellence, leadership, or dramatic improvement. | Read & Share Pride |
| **Homework & Study Task** | 📚 | Specific preparation notes (e.g., *Bring geometry set and compass for math tomorrow*). | Supervise Preparation |
| **Behavioral Observation**| ⚠️ | Disruptive behavior, missed assignments, or inattention in class. | Parent Reply & Guidance |
| **Dress Code / Uniform** | 👔 | Non-compliant school uniform, missing badge, or PE kit reminders. | Correct Attire |
| **Health & Medical Note** | 🩺 | Clinic visit, minor injury, or requests for medication administration. | Acknowledge & Confirm |

## 2. How Teachers Log a Communication Entry

1. Open **Teacher → My Class → Communication Book**.
2. Select the **Student** (or select multiple students for shared class reminders).
3. Choose the **Entry Type** (*Praise, Homework Note, Conduct, Uniform, Health*).
4. Enter the **Message Details**:
   - Write specific, constructive observations.
   - Attach photos or worksheets if necessary.
   - Check **Require Parent Acknowledgment / Signature** for behavioral or medical entries.
5. Click **Post to Communication Book**.
6. The parent receives an instant notification via their Mobile App, Telegram Bot, and SMS.

---

## 3. How Parents Review & Acknowledge Entries

1. Log in to the **Parent Portal** (`/parent`) and open **Communication Book**.
2. Unread entries are flagged with an unread dot.
3. Click on an entry to read teacher remarks.
4. For entries requiring confirmation:
   - Click the green **Acknowledge & Sign** button.
   - Optional: Type a direct reply message to the teacher (e.g., *"Thank you Teacher Aster, we have practiced the multiplication tables at home tonight and she is ready."*).
5. The homeroom teacher immediately sees a green **Acknowledged** checkmark on their class register.

---

## 4. Privacy & Archival Standards

- All communication book conversations are private between the designated parents and assigned teachers.
- School directors and academic supervisors have audit oversight to ensure professional communication standards.
- Full term records are archived and accessible for parent-teacher conferences and annual student portfolio reviews.

# School Announcements & Broadcasts

The **YeneSchool Announcements & Broadcast System** is the central megaphone for institutional communication. School directors and administrators can draft bilingual notices, attach documents, pin urgent alerts to user dashboards, and broadcast messages simultaneously across multiple delivery channels — including in-app feeds, SMS, Telegram channels, and mobile push notifications.

---

## 1. Announcement Types & Priority Levels

| Priority Level | Visual Badge | Target Audience Behavior | Delivery Channels |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Normal / General** | Blue Badge | Appears in regular dashboard announcement feeds. | In-App Feed, Portal Dashboard |
| **Important** | Amber Badge | Highlighted at top of dashboard feed; sent to Telegram. | In-App, Telegram Channel, Push Notification |
| **Urgent / Emergency** | Red Banner | Full-screen pop-up banner upon user login; requires acknowledgment. | In-App Popup, SMS Broadcast, Telegram, Push |

---

## 2. Step-by-Step: Creating a New Announcement

1. Navigate to **Communications → Announcements** (`/director/communications`).
2. Click the **+ Create Announcement** button.
3. Complete the announcement draft form:

| Field | Description | Best Practice & Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Announcement Title** | Clear headline visible in notification previews. | *Upcoming Midterm Exam Schedule & Revision Guidelines* |
| **Category** | Academic, Holiday, Finance, Emergency, Extracurricular. | Categorizes feed filters. |
| **Target Audience** | Choose recipients: *All Users*, *Teachers Only*, *Parents Only*, *Students Only*, or *Specific Grade Levels / Sections*. | Select *Grade 10 Parents* for national exam registration notices. |
| **Bilingual Body Text** | Rich-text message body supporting formatting, bullet points, and dual language (Amharic & English). | Click **AI Translate & Polish** to automatically format formal Amharic text. |
| **File Attachments** | PDF circulars, revised timetable matrices, or event posters (up to 10MB). | Attach official signed PDF circular. |
| **Pin Announcement** | Pin to the very top of recipient dashboards for a set duration. | Check to keep active until the event concludes. |
| **Delivery Channels** | Checkboxes for **In-App Portal**, **Telegram Channel**, **SMS Gateway**, and **Push Notification**. | Emergency alerts should enable SMS + Telegram. |

4. Preview the formatted announcement in desktop and mobile preview modes.
5. Choose **Publish Immediately** or **Schedule for Future Date/Time** (e.g., *Schedule for Monday 07:00 AM*).

---

## 3. Bilingual Drafting & Automated AI Polish

YeneSchool integrates an intelligent announcement drafter (`ai-announcement-drafter.service`) that eliminates drafting friction:
- **Instant Translation & Formatting:** Enter brief English bullet points and click **Draft Bilingual Notice**.
- The AI generates formal, culturally respectful **Amharic (ግዕዝ)** and **English** text with official institutional greetings and sign-offs.
- Automatically syncs Ethiopian Calendar dates (e.g., *ጥቅምት 15 ቀን 2016 ዓ.ም.*) with corresponding Gregorian dates (*October 25, 2023*).

---

## 4. Delivery Tracking & Analytics

After dispatching an announcement, view the live **Delivery Report**:
- **Total Recipients:** Total accounts targeted.
- **Read / Opened Rate:** Percentage of parents and staff who have viewed the notice.
- **SMS & Telegram Delivery Rate:** Delivery confirmations from telecom gateways (Ethio Telecom) and Telegram bots.
- **Unread Follow-Up:** Click **Resend SMS Reminder to Unread** to nudge parents who have not opened critical emergency circulars.

# Director Dashboard Overview

When you sign in as a Director, YeneSchool takes you directly to your personal command centre — the **Director Dashboard**. Everything you need to run your school is one click away from this page.

## The KPI Cards

At the top of the dashboard you will find six summary cards that update in real time:

| Card | What it shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Total Students** | Total enrolled students in the active academic year |
| **Total Teachers** | Number of active teaching staff |
| **Classes & Sections** | How many classes exist and how many sections they contain |
| **Attendance Today** | Today's school-wide present percentage, with a present / absent count |
| **Pending Enrollments** | Applications waiting for your approval |
| **Upcoming Exams** | Exams scheduled within the next 7 days |

## Charts Section

Below the KPI cards you will find four live charts:

- **Weekly Attendance Trend** — bar chart of daily attendance rates for the past week
- **Finance Summary** — fee collection vs. expected revenue
- **User Distribution** — breakdown of students, teachers, and parents
- **Class Distribution** — student count per grade level

A full-width **School Overview** radial chart summarises overall school health at a glance.

## Alerts & Quick Actions

Smart alerts appear between the header and the KPI cards whenever action is needed — for example, teachers who have not submitted attendance, or pending enrollments. You can dismiss any alert by clicking the × icon. Alerts return if the issue persists.

> [!TIP]
> Click **Refresh** (the circular arrow button in the top-right of the dashboard header) to reload all data without leaving the page.

## Navigation Menu

Use the left-hand sidebar to reach any part of the system:

- **Academic Years** — create and manage school years and terms
- **Class Sections** — manage grades, sections, and subject assignments
- **Timetable** — build and edit the weekly schedule
- **Assessments** — create exams and continuous assessment tasks
- **Attendance** — view school-wide attendance reports
- **Reports** — generate detailed reports and export data
- **Siren Management** — configure the school bell
- **Staff** — manage teaching and admin staff accounts

> [!NOTE]
> The dashboard supports both the **Ethiopian Calendar (EC)** and the **Gregorian Calendar**. Your school's calendar type is set in **Settings → School Settings**.

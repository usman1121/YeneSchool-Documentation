# Student Risk Engine & Early Warnings

The YeneSchool **Student Risk Engine** is an intelligent early-warning tool designed to help directors, headteachers, and counselors identify students who may need academic or pastoral intervention before problems escalate.

## How the Risk Score is Calculated

The system monitors four core indicators and aggregates them into a normalized score from **0 to 100**:

```
Risk Score = (Attendance × 35%) + (Academic Trend × 35%) + (Discipline × 15%) + (Fee Balance × 15%)
```

| Risk Level | Score Range | Badge Color | Recommended Action |
|------------|-------------|-------------|--------------------|
| **Low Risk** | 0 – 30 | Green | Routine monitoring |
| **Moderate Risk** | 31 – 60 | Yellow / Amber | Teacher check-in, mentor assignment |
| **High Risk** | 61 – 85 | Orange | Parent-teacher meeting, remedial support |
| **Critical Risk** | 86 – 100 | Red | Urgent intervention case conference |

---

## Viewing the Risk Dashboard

1. Navigate to **Director Dashboard** → **Risk & Analytics** or select **Student Risk** from the sidebar.
2. Filter the student cohort by:
   - **Academic Year & Term**
   - **Grade Level / Section**
   - **Risk Category** (Academic, Attendance, Discipline, or Combined)
3. The list shows each student's:
   - Current Risk Score & badge
   - 30-day risk trajectory (Improving ↗, Stable →, Worsening ↘)
   - Primary contributing factors (e.g., "3 consecutive unexcused absences", "Midterm math score dropped 25%")

---

## Taking Action on an At-Risk Student

Click on any student row in the risk table to open their **Intervention Profile**:

1. **Log a Note**: Add private counselor or administrator notes regarding student discussions.
2. **Assign a Remedial Plan**: Link the student to after-school tutoring or a study group.
3. **Notify Parent**: Trigger an SMS, push notification, or automated Telegram alert to the parent or guardian.
4. **Schedule Case Review**: Flag the student for discussion at the weekly staff meeting.

> [!NOTE]
> Risk scores recalculate every evening automatically when daily attendance and grading logs are processed by the background AI engine.

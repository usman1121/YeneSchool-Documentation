# Student Portal Overview

The **YeneSchool Student Portal** is designed to provide students with transparent, real-time access to their academic journey. From checking daily class schedules and downloading teacher lesson materials to taking online computer-based examinations, the portal puts all school activities in one accessible place.

---

## 1. Dashboard Layout & Summary Cards

When you log in to the Student Portal, the homepage presents an immediate summary of your school status:

| Widget / Card | What It Shows | Action Available |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Attendance Summary** | Your overall attendance rate (e.g., 96.5%) and total days present, late, or absent. | Click to view the full attendance calendar. |
| **Current GPA / Average** | Real-time cumulative average mark across all enrolled subjects for the active term. | Click to view subject-by-subject grade breakdown. |
| **Today's Classes** | Chronological list of periods, room numbers, subjects, and assigned teachers for today. | Click on a subject to open the digital lesson notes. |
| **Upcoming Assessments** | Upcoming quizzes, tests, midterm exams, and homework submission deadlines. | Click to view assignment instructions or open exam portal. |
| **School Announcements** | Official notices, holiday schedules, and event reminders broadcast by the school director. | Read full announcement text and download attachments. |

---

## 2. Navigating the Student Portal

The left navigation menu provides quick access to core student modules:

1. **My Schedule / Timetable:** View your weekly period grid from Monday through Friday.
2. **Lessons & Notes:** Browse lecture notes, slides, PDF textbooks, and multimedia materials shared by your teachers.
3. **Assignments & Homework:** Download homework briefs, view due dates, and upload digital submissions.
4. **Grades & Report Cards:** Inspect continuous assessment marks (quizzes, midterms, projects) and download signed terminal report cards.
5. **Online Examinations:** Access timed computer-based tests and practice quizzes.
6. **Transport & Bus Route:** Check your assigned school bus number, pickup time, and driver contact info.

> [!TIP]
> You can access the student portal from any smartphone browser, tablet, or laptop. When saved to your home screen as a Progressive Web App (PWA), your class schedule and downloaded lesson notes remain accessible even when offline.

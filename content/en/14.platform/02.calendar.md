# Ethiopian & Gregorian Calendar

YeneSchool is built for Ethiopian schools and fully supports the **Ethiopian Calendar (EC)**. All dates — attendance records, fee due dates, timetables, and report card terms — can be displayed in either EC or Gregorian (GC) format depending on your school's setting.

## How Calendars Are Configured

Your school administrator sets the preferred calendar type in **School Settings**. Most Ethiopian government and private schools use the Ethiopian Calendar by default.

> [!NOTE]
> The Ethiopian Calendar is approximately 7–8 years behind the Gregorian Calendar and has 13 months: 12 months of 30 days each, plus a short 13th month (Pagume) of 5 or 6 days.

## Ethiopian Calendar Month Names

| EC Month | Approximate GC Equivalent |
|---|---|
| መስከረም (Meskerem) | September – October |
| ጥቅምት (Tikmit) | October – November |
| ኅዳር (Hidar) | November – December |
| ታኅሣሥ (Tahsas) | December – January |
| ጥር (Tir) | January – February |
| የካቲት (Yekatit) | February – March |
| መጋቢት (Megabit) | March – April |
| ሚያዝያ (Miyazia) | April – May |
| ግንቦት (Ginbot) | May – June |
| ሰኔ (Sene) | June – July |
| ሐምሌ (Hamle) | July – August |
| ነሐሴ (Nehase) | August – September |
| ጳጉሜ (Pagume) | September |

## How Academic Terms Map to Calendar Months

Ethiopian schools typically run three terms aligned to the EC academic year starting in Meskerem (September GC):

| Term | EC Months | Approx. GC Period |
|---|---|---|
| Term 1 (1ኛ ሴሚስተር) | Meskerem – Hidar | Sep – Dec |
| Term 2 (2ኛ ሴሚስተር) | Tahsas – Megabit | Jan – Apr |
| Term 3 (3ኛ ሴሚስተር) | Miyazia – Sene | Apr – Jul |

> [!TIP]
> If you ever see a date that looks incorrect, check whether your school is using Ethiopian or Gregorian calendar mode. The date display changes instantly when the administrator switches the setting.

# Payment Receipts

Every payment recorded in YeneSchool automatically generates a **sequentially numbered receipt** (e.g., `R-2024-001`). Receipts are stored as PDFs and can be reprinted or downloaded at any time.

## Finding a Receipt

### By Recent Transactions

1. On the Finance dashboard, click the **Recent Transactions** tab.
2. Use the **Search** box above the table to type a student name, receipt number, or transaction reference.
3. The table filters in real time. Locate the payment row you need.
4. Click the **Receipt** icon or receipt number in that row to open the receipt.

### By Student Name

1. Click **Record Payment** to open the payment dialog.
2. Search for the student and select them.
3. Their previously recorded payments appear in the payment history section below the fee selector.
4. Click any receipt number to open and download that receipt.

## Viewing and Downloading a Receipt

1. Once the receipt is open, it displays in PDF format in a new browser tab.
2. Click the **Download** button (or use your browser's download icon) to save the PDF locally.
3. To print, press **Ctrl+P** (Windows/Linux) or **Cmd+P** (Mac) while the PDF is open.

> [!TIP]
> Receipts include the school name, student name, student ID, fee type, amount paid, payment method, date (in Ethiopian Calendar), and the finance officer's name.

> [!NOTE]
> Receipt numbers are sequential **per school** and never reused — even if a payment is reversed, the original receipt number is retired.

> [!WARNING]
> If a receipt shows an incorrect amount due to a data-entry error, do **not** delete it. Contact your administrator to initiate a payment reversal. A corrected receipt will be issued with a new number.

# Finance Dashboard Overview

The Finance dashboard is your central hub for managing all school revenue and fee activity. When you log in as a Finance Officer, the dashboard loads automatically for the active academic year.

## Key Metrics

The top row of the dashboard displays four summary cards:

| Card | What It Shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Total Revenue** | All payments collected for the selected academic year / term |
| **Collected Today** | Payments recorded since midnight (school local time) |
| **Outstanding Balance** | Total amount still owed across all enrolled students |
| **Total Discounts** | Sum of all approved discount amounts applied this period |

Below the summary cards, three student-count indicators show how many students are **Fully Paid**, **Partially Paid**, and **Unpaid**.

## Revenue Chart

The **Revenue Trend** area chart visualises payment collection over time. Use the **Daily / Weekly / Monthly** toggle above the chart to change the grouping.

> [!TIP]
> Switch to **Monthly** view at the end of each Ethiopian month to spot collection trends quickly.

## Tabs — Transactions & Outstanding Balances

Two tabs sit below the chart:

- **Recent Transactions** — a searchable, paginated list of every payment recorded, showing student name, receipt number, payment method, amount, and date.
- **Outstanding Balances** — a list of students with unpaid or partially paid fees. Filter by status (Paid / Partial / Unpaid) or search by student name.

## Quick Actions

Two buttons appear in the top-right corner of the page:

1. **Record Payment** — opens the payment dialog to log a new fee payment.
2. **Add Fee Structure** — opens the fee-structure creation dialog.

> [!NOTE]
> All amounts on the Finance dashboard are displayed in **ETB (Ethiopian Birr)**. The calendar uses the **Ethiopian Calendar (EC)** by default unless your school has switched to Gregorian.

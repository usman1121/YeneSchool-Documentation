# Multi-Branch & Campus Comparison

For educational organizations operating multiple campuses or branches (e.g., *Main Campus, Bole Branch, CMC Branch, Hawassa Campus*), YeneSchool enables centralized oversight without administrative bottlenecking.

---

## 1. Branch Comparison Grid

Navigate to **Owner → Branch Comparison** to review side-by-side performance indicators:

| Branch Name | Enrolled Students | Capacity Used | Fee Collection % | Avg Pass Rate | Staff Count |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

## 2. Unified Financial Rollup

- **Consolidated Cashflow:** View combined fee collections, bank balances, and operational expenditures in a single view.
- **Inter-Branch Benchmarking:** Identify high-performing campuses and transfer best pedagogical and financial practices to developing branches.
- **Unified Brand Standards:** Ensure consistent grading policies, attendance thresholds, and report card branding across all locations.

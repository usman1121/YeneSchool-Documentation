# Student Portal

The Student Portal gives students access to their academic information — timetable, grades, attendance, and assignments.

## Dashboard

When you log in as a Student, you'll see:
- **Today's Classes** — Your schedule for today
- **Upcoming Exams** — Exams scheduled in the next 7 days
- **Assignments Due** — Pending assignments
- **Grades Summary** — Your overall GPA
- **Attendance Rate** — Your attendance percentage

## Viewing Grades

1. Navigate to **Grades** in the sidebar
2. Select the term and subject
3. View marks, GPA, and class rank

## Viewing Attendance

1. Navigate to **Attendance** in the sidebar
2. View daily and monthly attendance records
3. See attendance status with color-coded indicators

## Viewing Assignments

1. Navigate to **Assignments** in the sidebar
2. See all assigned tasks with due dates
3. Submit assignments directly from the portal

> [!TIP]
> Your student account is created automatically during enrollment. Contact the Registrar if you don't have login credentials.

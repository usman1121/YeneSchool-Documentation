# AI Teaching Assistant & Quiz Generator

YeneSchool integrates a contextual **AI Teaching Copilot** into every teacher's workflow to save educators hours of administrative and preparation time each week.

> [!TIP] **Unified AI Intelligence Hub**
> For full technical details on prompt libraries, multi-turn data tools, and multilingual support, visit the [Interactive AI Assistant Guide](/en/ai/overview).

---

## 1. Generating Quiz Questions & Formative Assessments

1. Open the **AI Assistant** widget from the top navigation bar or press `Cmd + K` / `Ctrl + K`.
2. Select the **Quiz Generator** template.
3. Provide your parameters:
   - **Topic / Unit**: e.g., *Newtonian Laws of Motion & Friction*
   - **Grade Level**: *Grade 11*
   - **Question Types**: Multiple Choice, Short Answer, True/False, or Essay
   - **Cognitive Level**: Knowledge recall, Application, or Critical Thinking
   - **Language**: English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Somali, or Tigrinya
4. Click **Generate Questions**.
5. Review, edit inline, and click **Export to Assessment** to insert directly into an exam or homework assignment.

---

## 2. Fast-Track 5E Lesson Plan Generation

Prompt the AI with your curriculum standard or textbook unit:

```
Draft a 45-minute 5E lesson plan for Grade 8 Chemistry on 'Acids and Bases'.
Include:
1. 5-minute Engage hook using common Ethiopian household items (lemon juice, soap)
2. 15-minute Explore group litmus paper testing activity
3. 15-minute Explain pH scale concepts on blackboard
4. 5-minute Elaborate real-world agricultural soil acidity connection
5. 5-minute Evaluate exit ticket diagnostic quiz
```

The AI automatically formats the response into the YeneSchool lesson builder structure ready for 1-click publishing.

## 3. Differentiated Student Feedback & Remedial Plans

Use the AI to draft constructive, personalized report card comments based on a student's grade trend and attendance history:
- Highlight individual student strengths and notable improvements.
- Suggest tailored remedial study steps without repetitive phrasing.

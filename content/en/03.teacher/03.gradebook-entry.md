# Gradebook & Continuous Assessments

The YeneSchool **Gradebook** allows teachers to enter scores across multiple assessment categories (quizzes, homework, lab reports, midterm exams, and final exams) with automatic weighting calculations.

---

## Navigating the Gradebook Grid

1. Go to **Teacher Dashboard** → **Gradebook**.
2. Select:
   - **Academic Year & Term** (e.g., *2017 E.C. Semester 1*)
   - **Class & Section** (e.g., *Grade 10 - Section B*)
   - **Subject** (e.g., *Physics*)
3. The interactive spreadsheet grid opens displaying:
   - Student Name and ID
   - Assessment columns (e.g., Quiz 1 [10%], Test 1 [20%], Midterm [30%], Final Exam [40%])
   - Live **Cumulative Total** and **Letter Grade**

---

## Entering Marks

- **Keyboard Navigation**: Use `Tab`, `Enter`, or arrow keys to move quickly down or across columns.
- **Auto-Save**: Changes are saved automatically as you type.
- **Bulk Fill**: Apply a default score to all students or clear columns easily.
- **Import from Excel**: If you keep scores in an offline spreadsheet, click **Import Marks from CSV/Excel** to upload directly into the gradebook.

---

## Submitting & Locking Grades

When end-of-term grading is complete:

1. Click **Review & Validate**.
2. Check the class average curve and verify there are no missing grades.
3. Click **Submit to Director / Registrar for Approval**.
4. Once approved, grades are locked and automatically populated into the official student report cards.

> [!TIP]
> You can add qualitative remarks (e.g., "Demonstrates exceptional critical thinking") directly in the comment field for inclusion on report cards.

# Risk & Analytics

The Risk & Analytics module helps identify students who may be at risk based on attendance, grades, discipline, and fee signals.

## Risk Engine

YeneSchool's early-warning risk engine monitors multiple signals:

| Signal | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Attendance** | Low attendance rate triggers a warning |
| **Grades** | Declining grades trigger a warning |
| **Discipline** | Multiple discipline incidents trigger a warning |
| **Fees** | Outstanding fees trigger a warning |

## Risk Dashboard

1. Navigate to **Risk & Analytics** in the sidebar
2. View the **Risk Overview** dashboard
3. See at-risk students with their risk level
4. Drill down to see individual risk details
5. Track risk trends over time

## Risk Categories

- **Low Risk** — One signal triggered
- **Medium Risk** — Two signals triggered
- **High Risk** — Three or more signals triggered

## Trend Tracking

- View historical risk data
- Compare trends across classes and terms
- Export risk reports as CSV

> [TIP]
> The Director Dashboard shows risk indicators in the KPI cards. Click on at-risk students to see their full profile.

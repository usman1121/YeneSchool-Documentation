# Teacher Dashboard Overview

When you sign in as a Teacher, YeneSchool takes you straight to your personal dashboard. Everything is designed around your teaching day.

## What You See

### Welcome Header
The header greets you by first name and shows today's date. A **Current Period** badge in the top-right corner tells you which class period is active right now — no need to check the timetable.

### Three KPI Cards

| Card | What It Shows |
|------|---------------|
| **Today's Classes** | Number of periods you are scheduled for today |
| **Attendance Pending** | Periods still needing attendance submission (highlighted in amber when non-zero) |
| **Exams to Grade** | Student submissions waiting for marks |

Clicking any card takes you directly to the related page.

### Today's Schedule
A list of every class period for today, showing subject, grade, section, room, and time. A **LIVE** badge pulses red on the period currently in progress. Each row has a **Take Attendance** button so you can jump straight to marking without going through the menu.

### Quick Shortcuts
Four tiles in the sidebar give one-tap access to the most-used workspaces: **Attendance**, **Enter Grades**, **Lesson Plans**, and **Assignments**.

### Announcements
School announcements addressed to teachers appear below the shortcuts, newest first.

## Quick Parent Alert
Click the red **Quick Parent Alert** button in the header to instantly notify parents that homework or a worksheet has been assigned — without opening the Lessons page.

> [!TIP]
> If you have no classes today, YeneSchool suggests using the time to review student submissions or message parents — and links you to the Full Timetable.

## Navigation Menu
Use the left-hand sidebar to reach:
- **Attendance** — Mark and view attendance
- **Enter Grades** — Enter and manage student grades
- **Lesson Plans** — Create and manage lesson plans
- **Assignments** — Create and manage assignments
- **Exams** — Create and manage exams
- **Discipline** — Manage student discipline records
- **Messaging** — Communicate with students and parents

> [!NOTE]
> The dashboard supports both the **Ethiopian Calendar (EC)** and the **Gregorian Calendar (GC)**.

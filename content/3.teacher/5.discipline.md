# Student Discipline & Behavior Logs

The **Discipline & Behavior** module enables teachers to record both disciplinary infractions and positive commendations to maintain a transparent, fair, and supportive school environment.

---

## Logging a Behavioral Incident

1. Go to **Teacher Dashboard** → **Discipline** → **Log Incident**.
2. Select the **Student** (or multiple students if an incident involved a group).
3. Fill in the incident record:
   - **Incident Type**: e.g., *Classroom Disruption*, *Unprepared for Class*, *Academic Dishonesty*, *Property Damage*, *Positive Commendation / Leadership*
   - **Severity Level**: Minor (Teacher-handled), Moderate (Counselor referral), Major (Director escalation)
   - **Date, Time & Location**: e.g., *Science Lab, Period 3*
   - **Detailed Description**: Objective description of what occurred and immediate actions taken.
4. Check **Notify Parent / Guardian** if an immediate SMS or push alert should be dispatched.
5. Click **Submit Record**.

---

## Reviewing Incident History

- View a student's past behavioral record before parent-teacher conferences.
- Track disciplinary resolution statuses (*Pending Investigation*, *Parent Contacted*, *Detention Served*, *Resolved*).
- School directors and counselors receive instant notifications for any *Major* category escalation.

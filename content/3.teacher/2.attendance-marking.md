# Attendance Marking (Online & Offline)

Recording accurate daily and period attendance is one of the primary workflows for teachers in YeneSchool. The system is designed to allow marking an entire class of 40+ students in under 30 seconds.

---

## Marking Attendance Step-by-Step

1. Log in to the **Teacher Portal** and select **Attendance** from the sidebar or tap **Mark Attendance Today** on your dashboard.
2. Choose your **Class / Section** and **Period / Subject** (if your school tracks period-by-period attendance).
3. The student list opens with all students defaulting to **Present (P)**.
4. Tap/click on individual students to toggle status:
   - **Green (P)**: Present
   - **Red (A)**: Absent (triggers optional SMS notification to parents)
   - **Yellow (L)**: Late / Tardy (enter minutes late if required)
   - **Blue (E)**: Excused Absence (with optional medical or note attachment)
5. Review the summary counts at the bottom and click **Submit Attendance**.

---

## Offline Attendance Workflow

YeneSchool operates as an **Offline-First Progressive Web App (PWA)**. If your classroom loses internet connectivity or power:

1. Continue using YeneSchool as normal on your phone, tablet, or laptop.
2. A subtle **Offline Mode** indicator will appear in the top bar.
3. Mark and submit attendance as usual. The records are safely encrypted in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB).
4. As soon as your device reconnects to Wi-Fi or cellular data, all attendance records **sync automatically in the background**.

> [!NOTE]
> Offline changes are timestamped locally so arrival times and period markers remain 100% accurate when synced to the server.

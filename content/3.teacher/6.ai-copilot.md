# AI Teaching Assistant & Quiz Generator

YeneSchool integrates a contextual **AI Teaching Copilot** to save educators hours of administrative and preparation time each week.

---

## 1. Generating Quiz Questions

1. Open the **AI Assistant** widget from the top navigation bar or press `Ctrl + /`.
2. Select the **Quiz Generator** action template.
3. Provide your parameters:
   - **Topic**: e.g., *Newtonian Laws of Motion & Friction*
   - **Grade Level**: *Grade 11*
   - **Question Types**: Multiple Choice, Short Answer, True/False, or Essay
   - **Difficulty Level**: Easy, Moderate, Challenging
   - **Language**: English, Amharic, Afaan Oromo, etc.
4. Click **Generate Questions**.
5. Review, edit, and click **Export to Assessment** to insert directly into an exam or assignment.

---

## 2. Drafting Lesson Plans

Prompt the AI with your curriculum standard or topic:

```
Draft a 45-minute interactive lesson plan for Grade 8 Chemistry on 'Acids and Bases'.
Include:
1. 5-minute hook / warm-up activity
2. 20-minute core explanation with real-world Ethiopian examples
3. 15-minute group activity
4. 5-minute exit ticket question
```

The AI formats the response directly into the YeneSchool lesson builder structure.

---

## 3. Differentiated Student Feedback

Use the AI to draft constructive, personalized report card comments based on a student's grade trend and attendance history without repetitive phrasing.

> [!TIP]
> The AI Copilot supports multilingual generation in Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Somali, and Arabic alongside English.

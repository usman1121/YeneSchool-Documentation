# Lessons & Assignment Management

Teachers can organize curriculum delivery, share digital study notes, and assign homework through the **Lessons & Assignments** module.

---

## 1. Creating a Lesson Plan

1. In the sidebar, select **Lessons** → **New Lesson**.
2. Fill in the lesson details:
   - **Title**: e.g., *Introduction to Photosynthesis & Plant Energy*
   - **Subject & Grade**: e.g., *Biology — Grade 9*
   - **Lesson Objectives**: Key learning outcomes for students
   - **Rich Content**: Type notes, insert formatting, diagrams, and math equations
   - **Attachments**: Upload PDFs, PowerPoint slides, or embed YouTube video links.
3. Choose **Publish Now** or **Save as Draft**.

---

## 2. Creating an Assignment

1. Go to **Assignments** → **Create Assignment**.
2. Configure:
   - **Target Class & Subject**
   - **Submission Deadline** (Date & Time)
   - **Maximum Marks** (e.g., 20 points)
   - **Submission Format**: File upload (PDF/Word/Image), online text entry, or physical paper turn-in.
3. Click **Publish Assignment**. Students and parents receive an instant notification.

---

## 3. Grading Submissions

1. Open the assignment page to view the **Submissions Tracker**:
   - Total Submitted vs. Pending vs. Overdue
2. Click on a student's submission to preview their submitted file in-app.
3. Enter the **Score** and optional **Feedback / Corrections**.
4. Click **Return to Student**. Grades can optionally sync directly into your gradebook continuous assessment column.

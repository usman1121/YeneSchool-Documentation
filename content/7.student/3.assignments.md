# Assignments & Digital Lessons

YeneSchool gives students 24/7 access to classroom materials, lecture slides, video links, and homework assignments published by teachers.

---

## 1. Accessing Lesson Notes

1. Click **Lessons & Materials** in the sidebar.
2. Select your **Subject** (e.g., *Mathematics, English, Biology, History*).
3. The lesson feed displays topics organized chronologically by academic term and unit.
4. Click on any topic to:
   - Read teacher lesson summaries and key definitions.
   - Download attached PDFs, Word documents, or presentation slides.
   - View recommended external links and educational videos.

---

## 2. Viewing and Submitting Homework

To submit an assignment:

1. Navigate to **Assignments** from the menu.
2. Filter by status: **Pending**, **Submitted**, or **Graded**.
3. Click on a pending assignment to view:
   - **Instructions & Rubric:** Full task description and score breakdown.
   - **Deadline:** Submission closing date and time.
   - **Allowed File Formats:** (PDF, DOCX, JPG, PNG, etc.).
4. Complete your homework and prepare your document or photos of handwritten work.
5. In the **Submit Assignment** box:
   - Drag and drop your file or click **Browse Files**.
   - Optional: Add a brief note for the teacher.
6. Click **Upload & Turn In**.

---

## 3. Assignment Statuses & Teacher Feedback

| Status Badge | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Pending` | Assignment is open and waiting for your submission before the deadline. |
| `Submitted` | You have successfully submitted your work; awaiting teacher review. |
| `Graded` | Teacher has reviewed your work. Click to view score, rubric points, and written remarks. |
| `Overdue` | The deadline has passed without a submission. Check if late submissions are accepted. |

> [!TIP]
> Always verify that your uploaded file preview is clear and legible before clicking submit, especially when uploading photos of handwritten homework notebooks.

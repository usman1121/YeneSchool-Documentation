# Online Examination Portal

The **YeneSchool Online Examination Portal** allows students to complete secure, timed computer-based assessments, midterms, and practice quizzes directly from their browser or mobile device.

---

## 1. Before Starting an Exam

1. Navigate to **Online Exams** from the student sidebar.
2. Review the list of **Available Exams**, noting:
   - **Subject & Title:** (e.g., *Grade 10 Physics Midterm Exam*)
   - **Scheduled Window:** Start time and expiration cutoff date.
   - **Duration:** Allowed time in minutes (e.g., 60 Minutes).
   - **Total Questions & Passing Mark:** Total score possible and minimum pass score.
3. Ensure you have a stable device battery and click **Start Exam**.
4. Read the exam instructions and honor pledge modal, then confirm to enter the test environment.

---

## 2. Exam Interface & Features

| Interface Component | Description & Function |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Countdown Timer** | High-contrast clock in the top header displaying remaining minutes and seconds. Color turns amber at 5 minutes and red at 1 minute. |
| **Question Navigation Palette** | Numbered grid of buttons representing every question: Gray (Unanswered), Blue (Answered), Yellow Flag (Marked for review). |
| **Question View** | Displays question text, supporting diagrams/images, and choices (Multiple Choice, True/False, or Short Answer). |
| **Flag Question Button** | Allows you to flag difficult questions so you can easily return to them before submitting. |
| **Auto-Save Indicator** | Green checkmark confirming each answer is instantly recorded to the server and local device memory. |

---

## 3. Submitting the Exam

1. When you answer all questions, review your flagged items via the Question Palette.
2. Click the green **Submit Exam** button in the bottom right corner.
3. A confirmation dialog will display total questions answered vs unanswered and remaining time.
4. Click **Confirm & Submit Final Answers**.

> [!NOTE]
> If your timer expires while taking the exam, the system automatically saves your current selections and submits the exam on your behalf so no answered questions are lost.

---

## 4. Viewing Exam Results

- **Objective Exams (MCQ & True/False):** Once submitted, your score and percentage are calculated immediately. You can review correct answers and teacher explanations if the instructor has enabled post-exam review.
- **Subjective Exams (Short Answer / Essays):** Scores will be published after the teacher grades open-ended responses. You will receive an in-app notification when the final grade is posted.

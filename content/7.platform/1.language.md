# Language & Localisation

YeneSchool supports five languages to serve Ethiopia's diverse communities. You can switch the entire interface — menus, labels, buttons, and notifications — to your preferred language at any time.

## Supported Languages

| Language | Code | Script |
|---|---|---|
| English | `en` | Latin |
| አማርኛ (Amharic) | `am` | Ethiopic (Ge'ez) |
| Afaan Oromo | `om` | Latin |
| Soomaali (Somali) | `so` | Latin |
| العربية (Arabic) | `ar` | Arabic (RTL) |

> [!NOTE]
> Arabic displays right-to-left (RTL). The entire layout mirrors automatically when Arabic is selected.

## How to Switch Language

1. Log in to YeneSchool.
2. Click your **profile avatar** or name in the top-right corner of any page.
3. Select **Language** (or **ቋንቋ** in Amharic) from the dropdown menu.
4. Choose your preferred language from the list.
5. The page reloads immediately in the selected language — no further action needed.

> [!TIP]
> Your language preference is saved to your account. You will see the same language the next time you log in, even on a different device.

## What Changes When You Switch

- All navigation menus and sidebar labels
- Page titles and form field labels
- System notifications and alerts
- Date and number formats (where applicable)
- Report card and document headings

> [!NOTE]
> Some custom content entered by your school (such as teacher remarks or school announcements) may remain in the language it was originally written in. AI-powered translation of dynamic content requires the school administrator to enable the translation feature.

# Notifications

YeneSchool keeps you informed through multiple channels so you never miss an important update about your child or the school.

## Notification Channels

| Channel | Description |
|---|---|
| **In-App** | Bell icon in the top navigation bar; shows history of all alerts |
| **Push Notification** | Instant alerts on your phone or browser, even when the app is closed |
| **Email** | Summaries and important notices sent to your registered email address |
| **SMS** | Critical alerts (e.g., absences) sent to your registered mobile number |
| **Telegram** | Alerts delivered to your Telegram account if the school has enabled the integration |

## Events That Trigger Notifications

| Event | Who Is Notified |
|---|---|
| Child marked absent | Parent |
| New grades published | Student & Parent |
| Fee payment due | Parent |
| Exam schedule released | Student & Parent |
| New school announcement | All users |
| Teacher message received | Parent |

## Setting Up Push Notifications

1. Log in to YeneSchool in your browser or the installed app.
2. When prompted, click **Allow** on the browser permission dialog to enable push notifications.
3. If you missed the prompt, go to **Profile → Notification Preferences** and click **Enable Push Notifications**.

## Managing Your Preferences

1. Click your **profile avatar** in the top-right corner.
2. Select **Notification Preferences**.
3. Toggle each channel (Push, Email, SMS, Telegram) on or off.
4. Choose which events you want to be notified about per channel.
5. Click **Save Preferences**.

> [!TIP]
> Enable SMS for absences even if you rarely use the app — SMS works without a smartphone and ensures you are always informed when your child does not arrive at school.

> [!NOTE]
> SMS and Telegram notifications depend on your school's configuration. If these options are greyed out, the school has not yet activated those channels. Contact your school administrator.

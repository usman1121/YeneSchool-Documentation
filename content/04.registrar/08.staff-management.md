# Staff Management

Manage all staff accounts — create, edit, deactivate, and assign roles and permissions. Every staff member has a defined role that determines their access to the system.

## Creating Staff Accounts

1. Navigate to **Staff** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Staff**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Name** — Full name
   - **Email** — Login email
   - **Role** — Staff, Registrar, Teacher, Director, etc.
   - **Branch** — Which branch the staff belongs to
   - **Phone** — Contact phone number
4. Click **Save**

## Staff Roles

| Role | Permissions |
|------|-------------|
| **Teacher** | Mark attendance, enter grades, create lessons |
| **Registrar** | Student enrollment, bulk import, ID cards, report cards |
| **Director** | Academic management, staff management, school-wide data |
| **Finance Officer** | Fee structures, payment recording, reports |
| **Supervisor** | Oversight across multiple branches |
| **Storekeeper** | Inventory management |

## Managing Permissions

1. Open a staff account
2. Scroll to **Permissions**
3. Enable or disable specific permissions
4. Click **Save**

> [!TIP]
> Use RBAC (Role-Based Access Control) to manage permissions efficiently. Each role has predefined permissions that can be customized.

## Deactivating Accounts

To remove a staff member's access:
1. Open their account
2. Toggle **Active** to **Inactive**
3. All their data remains in the system but they can no longer log in

# Student Management

The **Student List** is your central hub for finding, reviewing, and updating any student record in the school.

## Accessing the Student List

From the Registrar Dashboard, click **Manage Students**. This opens the full student list at `/list/students`.

## Searching and Filtering

Use the search bar at the top to find students by:

- Full name or part of a name
- Student code (admission number)
- Email address
- Phone number

You can also filter the list using the available dropdowns:

| Filter | Options |
|---|---|
| Academic Year | Select from all configured years |
| Grade / Class | Filter by a specific grade level |
| Section | Narrow to a specific section |
| Enrollment Status | Active, Pending, Approved, Rejected |
| Gender | Male / Female |

> [!TIP]
> Combine a grade filter with a section filter to see exactly who is in a specific class, useful when preparing class lists or attendance sheets.

## Editing a Student Profile

1. Click any student's name or row to open their **profile page**.
2. Click **Edit** (pencil icon) to enter edit mode.
3. You can update:
   - Personal details — name, date of birth, gender, phone, email
   - Fayda Number (FAN)
   - Guardian information — parent name, phone, relationship
   - Profile photo — upload a new photo or replace the existing one
4. Click **Save** to apply changes. The student's login account is updated automatically.

> [!WARNING]
> Changing a student's phone number will change their login username. Notify the student of the new login details.

## Viewing a Student's History

Click **View** on a student profile to see their complete history in YeneSchool:

| Tab | What You See |
|---|---|
| Profile | Personal details, photo, guardian info, student code |
| Attendance | Daily attendance records across all terms |
| Grades | Subject marks and report card summaries per term |
| Fees | Fee invoices, payment history, outstanding balance |
| Documents | Uploaded documents — photos, transcripts, certificates |
| Enrollment | Current class, section, and enrollment status per year |

## Managing Documents

1. Open a student's profile and go to the **Documents** tab.
2. Click **Upload Document** to attach files such as birth certificates, previous school transcripts, or photos.
3. Each document has a type label (e.g., `PHOTO`, `TRANSCRIPT`, `CERTIFICATE`) so it is easy to search and filter later.

> [!NOTE]
> Documents uploaded here are also visible to the student and their linked parent/guardian once published.

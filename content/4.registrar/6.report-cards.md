# Generate Report Cards

Report cards summarise a student's academic performance for a term — marks per subject, overall percentage, rank in class, and attendance. In YeneSchool, report cards go through a three-stage lifecycle before students can see them.

## Report Card Lifecycle

| Stage | Who Can See It | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **DRAFT** | Registrar, Director | Grades are entered but not final |
| **PUBLISHED** | Student, Parent, Teacher | Official — visible to all linked users |
| **ARCHIVED** | Read-only for all | Finalised historical record |

> [!NOTE]
> Only **PUBLISHED** report cards are visible to students and parents. Always review carefully before publishing.

## Step 1 — Select the Term and Classes

1. Go to **Director → Report Cards** from the side menu (the Registrar has access to this section).
2. Select the **Academic Year** and **Term** (Semester 1, Semester 2, or the configured term name).
3. Select one or more **Classes** to generate report cards for. You can select an entire grade level at once.

## Step 2 — Generate Report Cards

1. Click **Generate Report Cards**. YeneSchool pulls grades from the Gradebook and attendance from the Attendance module for each student in the selected classes.
2. Each card is created in **DRAFT** status automatically.
3. A summary shows how many cards were generated and whether any students had missing grades.

> [!WARNING]
> Report cards cannot be generated if teachers have not entered grades in the Gradebook for the selected term. Confirm with teachers that all marks are entered before proceeding.

## Step 3 — Review and Add Remarks

1. Click any student's name to open their draft report card.
2. Review marks, percentages, and rank.
3. Add or edit the **Teacher's Remarks** field if needed.

## Step 4 — Financial Clearance (Optional)

If your school has the financial clearance gate enabled, students with outstanding fee balances will be flagged. You can choose to:

- Hold their report card in DRAFT until fees are paid.
- Override the gate and publish anyway (requires Director approval at some schools).

## Step 5 — Publish

1. Select all the reviewed report cards (or click **Select All**).
2. Click **Publish**. The status changes to PUBLISHED and students and parents can now see them.

## Step 6 — Print or Download PDF

1. Select one or more published report cards.
2. Click **Print** to open the browser print dialog, or **Download PDF** to save a file.
3. For bulk printing an entire class, click **Print All** on the class report cards page.

> [!TIP]
> Use **Ethiopian Calendar** dates if your school operates on the EC. The report card printing respects the calendar type set in your school settings.

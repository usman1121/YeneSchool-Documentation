# Grade Promotion

At the end of each academic year, the Registrar processes **grade promotion** — moving eligible students to the next grade level and recording any students who are held back or retained. All decisions are stored in the promotion history for future reference.

## Navigate to Promotion

From the Registrar Dashboard, click **Promotion Decisions**. This opens the **Promotion** page under Director tools, which the Registrar can fully access.

## Promotion Criteria

YeneSchool validates three categories before a student can be promoted:

| Criterion | What Is Checked |
|---|---|
| **Grades** | Student's final term marks meet the passing threshold |
| **Attendance** | Attendance rate meets the school minimum (e.g., 75%) |
| **Fees** | No outstanding fee balance (if fee clearance is enabled) |

Students who meet all three criteria are marked **Eligible**. Others are flagged with the reason(s) they do not qualify.

> [!NOTE]
> Criteria thresholds are configured by your Director in School Settings. Contact your Director if you believe the thresholds are incorrect.

## Step 1 — Select Class and Academic Year

1. On the Promotion page, select the **Academic Year** you are promoting from (the year that is ending).
2. Select the **Class** (e.g., Grade 7) to process one grade at a time.
3. Click **Load Students** to see the full student list with their eligibility status.

## Step 2 — Review Student Eligibility

The promotion table shows every student with a clear status indicator:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Eligible | Meets all criteria — ready to promote |
| ⚠️ Flagged | Fails one or more criteria — review before deciding |
| ❌ Ineligible | Does not meet the minimum standard — recommend retention |

Click any flagged student to see the specific reason (e.g., `Mathematics mark below passing grade`, `Attendance 62% — below 75% threshold`).

## Step 3 — Make Promotion Decisions

1. For **eligible students**, select them all and click **Promote Selected**. Choose the target class (e.g., Grade 8) from the dropdown.
2. For **flagged students**, review each case and choose:
   - **Promote** — override and move to next grade (add a remark explaining the decision)
   - **Retain** — keep in current grade for another year (add a remark)
3. Every decision requires a short **Remark** (e.g., "Promoted by Director waiver — fees paid in instalments").

## Step 4 — Confirm and Record

1. Once all students have a decision, click **Confirm Promotions**.
2. YeneSchool creates a **Promotion Record** for each student, storing:
   - From class / To class
   - From academic year / To academic year
   - Status (PROMOTED or RETAINED)
   - Remark and timestamp
3. Promoted students are automatically assigned to their new class section at the start of the next academic year.

> [!WARNING]
> Promotion decisions **cannot be undone** after confirmation without Director intervention. Review carefully before clicking Confirm.

> [!TIP]
> Run the promotion process class by class — Grade 1 through your highest grade — to avoid missing any student groups.

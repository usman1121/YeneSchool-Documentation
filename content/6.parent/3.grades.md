# Viewing Grades

YeneSchool lets you see how your child is performing in every subject — including individual assessment scores — so you can have informed conversations with teachers and support your child at home.

## Opening the Grades Page

1. Log in and go to the **Parent Dashboard**.
2. From the sidebar, click **Grades**, or open your child's profile and select **Academic Results**.
3. Choose a **term** from the dropdown at the top right — or select **All Terms** for a combined view.

> [!WARNING]
> Grades are only visible if your child has no outstanding fee balance for that term. If results appear locked, contact the school finance office to resolve any pending payment.

## Understanding the Summary Cards

At the top of the Academic Results page you will see four summary cards:

| Card | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **GPA** | Grade Point Average on a 4.0 scale |
| **Rank** | Your child's position in their class |
| **Total Subjects** | Number of subjects assessed this term |
| **Overall Performance** | Excellent / Good / Needs Improvement |

## Subject-by-Subject Breakdown

The main table lists every subject with columns for each assessment type (Continuous Assessment, Mid-term, Final Exam) plus a **Total Score** and **Grade Letter**.

| Grade | Score Range | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 80 – 100% | Excellent |
| **B** | 60 – 79% | Good |
| **C** | 50 – 59% | Average |
| **D** | 40 – 49% | Below Average |
| **F** | 0 – 39% | Needs Improvement |

## Performance Chart

A radar chart on the right side of the page visualises your child's scores across all subjects at once. Subjects with a wider area on the chart are subjects where your child is performing well.

> [!TIP]
> Compare radar charts across different terms to spot subjects where your child is improving or needs extra attention.

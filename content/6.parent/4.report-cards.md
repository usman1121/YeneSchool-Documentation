# Report Cards

End-of-term report cards are published by the school once teachers have finalised all grades and the director has approved them. As a parent, you can view and download your child's report card directly from YeneSchool — no need to collect a paper copy.

## Viewing a Report Card

1. Log in and go to the **Parent Dashboard**.
2. From the sidebar, select **Grades** or your child's profile, then click **Academic Results**.
3. On the Academic Results page, use the **term selector** dropdown to pick the term whose report card you want to view.
4. The published report card for that term loads automatically, showing:
   - Subject names and assessment scores
   - Total score and grade letter per subject
   - GPA, class rank, and overall performance
   - Teacher and director remarks (if included)

> [!NOTE]
> If the report card is not yet visible, the school has not published it yet. Published report cards appear automatically once the school releases them — you may also receive a push notification.

## Downloading as PDF

1. Open the **Academic Results** page for your child.
2. Click the **Download PDF** button in the top-right corner.
3. The report card PDF is generated and saved to your device's downloads folder.

> [!TIP]
> The PDF is formatted for printing. Take it to any print shop to produce an official-looking paper copy if the school requires one for a file or application.

## Fee Payment Requirement

> [!WARNING]
> Report cards may be locked if your child has an outstanding fee balance for that term. The message **"Results are locked until fees are paid"** will appear instead of the scores. Visit the **Fees & Payments** section to settle any balance and then refresh the page.

## Checking for Top Rankings

If your child finishes in the **top 3 of their class**, YeneSchool shows a congratulations message the first time you open their results. The class rank is displayed on the summary card alongside GPA.

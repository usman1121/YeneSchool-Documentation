# Student Timetable

The Student Timetable shows your weekly class schedule. View it by day or by week to plan your classes.

## Viewing Your Timetable

1. Log in as a Student
2. Navigate to **Timetable** in the sidebar
3. Your timetable shows all classes for the week

## Timetable Features

- **Daily View** — See all classes for a single day
- **Weekly View** — See the full week at a glance
- **Current Period Indicator** — Shows which class is active right now
- **Room Information** — Know which classroom to go to
- **Teacher Information** — See who is teaching each period

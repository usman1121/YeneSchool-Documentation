# Viewing Attendance

YeneSchool lets parents monitor their child's attendance at any time — by individual day or across an entire month — without needing to call the school.

## Opening Attendance Records

1. Log in and go to your **Parent Dashboard**.
2. From the sidebar, select **Attendance**, or click **View Attendance** on your child's overview card.
3. The **Attendance Records** page opens showing the current month by default.
4. Use the **month** and **year** dropdowns at the top right to browse other months.

## Monthly Summary Cards

At the top of the page you will see five summary cards:

| Card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Total Days** | School days in the selected month |
| **Present** | Days your child attended |
| **Absent** | Days missed without a recorded excuse |
| **Late** | Days arrived after the official start time |
| **Attendance Rate** | Percentage of days attended (target: 90%) |

The coloured progress bar below the cards turns **green** above 90%, **yellow** between 75–89%, and **red** below 75%.

## Status Icons Explained

| Icon | Status | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| ✓ | Present | Green |
| ✗ | Absent | Red |
| ⏰ | Late | Yellow |
| ½ | Half Day | Orange |
| 📋 | Leave (excused) | Blue |
| 🎉 | Holiday | Purple |

## Attendance Calendar

Below the summary cards, a full **monthly calendar** displays each school day coloured by status. Click any date to see any teacher remarks recorded for that day.

## Absence Alerts

> [!IMPORTANT]
> YeneSchool sends an automated notification when your child is marked absent. Make sure push notifications are enabled on your device so you receive these alerts immediately — see [Notifications](4.notifications) for setup steps.

# Parent Dashboard Overview

The **Parent Dashboard** is your central hub for staying informed about your child's school life. Once you log in with your parent account, you will see a personalised view showing only the children linked to your profile.

## What You Can Do

| Section | What You See |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Attendance rate, upcoming exams, fee balance at a glance |
| **Attendance** | Daily records and monthly calendar for each child |
| **Grades & Results** | Subject scores, GPA, class rank per term |
| **Report Cards** | Published end-of-term report cards to download |
| **Fees & Payments** | Total fees due, amount paid, outstanding balance |
| **Messages** | Communication book with your child's teachers |
| **Announcements** | School notices and events |

## Switching Between Children

If you have more than one child enrolled, use the **child selector** at the top of the dashboard. Click the dropdown showing your child's name and class to switch between children instantly.

> [!NOTE]
> Your account is linked to your children by the school Registrar during enrolment. If a child is missing from your dashboard, contact the school office to request the link.

## Getting Started

1. Open YeneSchool in your browser or mobile app and log in with your parent credentials.
2. The dashboard loads automatically showing your first linked child's summary.
3. Use the top menu or sidebar to navigate to Attendance, Grades, Fees, or Messages.
4. Tap the child name dropdown to switch to another child if you have multiple.

> [!TIP]
> Enable push notifications so you receive instant alerts when your child is absent or when new grades are published — no need to log in every day to check.

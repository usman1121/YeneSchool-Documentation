# User Profile & Telegram Bot Connection

Every user on YeneSchool (Directors, Teachers, Registrars, Finance Officers, Parents, and Students) has a dedicated **User Profile & Account Settings** page. Through this center, users can manage their personal credentials, configure multi-channel notifications, and pair their account with the **Official YeneSchool Telegram Bot**.

---

## 1. Accessing Your Profile & Account Settings

1. Click on your **User Avatar / Name** in the top-right corner of the navigation bar.
2. Select **My Profile & Settings** (`/profile`).
3. The profile management console is organized into four main tabs:
   - **Personal Info:** Name, contact phone, official email, profile photo, and biography.
   - **Security & Password:** Change password, manage active sessions, and configure Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
   - **Display & Language:** Preferred interface language (Amharic, Afaan Oromo, Somali, Arabic, English) and default calendar (Ethiopian E.C. vs Gregorian G.C.).
   - **Notifications & Integrations:** Telegram pairing, SMS alerts, email summaries, and push notifications.

---

## 2. Connecting Your Account to the Telegram Bot

Connecting your Telegram account allows you to receive instant alerts (such as child absence alerts, grade postings, fee receipts, and director briefings) with zero SMS costs and instant delivery.

```
[Click Connect Telegram] ──> [Open Bot Deep Link / Scan QR] ──> [Click /start in Telegram] ──> [Account Linked Successfully]
```

### Step-by-Step Pairing Guide:
1. In **Profile → Notifications & Integrations**, scroll to the **Telegram Notifications** section.
2. If your account is not linked, you will see a blue **Connect Telegram Bot** button.
3. Click **Connect Telegram Bot**. The system generates a secure one-time pairing link (or 6-digit OTP token):
   - **On Mobile Devices:** Click the direct deep link (e.g., `https://t.me/YeneSchoolBot?start=AUTH_TOKEN`). It will automatically launch the Telegram app.
   - **On Desktop / Laptop:** Open Telegram, search for `@YeneSchoolBot` (or your school's custom bot username), and type `/start <YOUR_6_DIGIT_TOKEN>`.
4. The Telegram bot will immediately respond:
   > *"Welcome [Your Name]! Your Telegram account has been securely linked to your YeneSchool profile at [School Name]. You will now receive real-time updates here."*
5. Refresh your profile page: the status badge will update to **Linked (Active)**.

---

## 3. Customizing Telegram Notification Preferences

Once linked, customize which specific event categories you wish to receive via Telegram:

| Notification Category | Who It Is For | What Triggers It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Attendance Alerts** | Parents & Homeroom Teachers | Real-time alert when a child is marked Absent or Late in morning roll-call. |
| **Exam Marks & Grades** | Parents & Students | Notification when a teacher publishes midterm or terminal exam scores. |
| **Fee Invoices & Receipts**| Parents & Finance Officers | Digital payment confirmation and upcoming installment due dates. |
| **School Announcements** | All Users | Emergency school closures, holiday schedules, and event reminders. |
| **Daily Director Briefing**| Directors & Supervisors | Morning AI executive operational summary delivered at 07:30 AM. |

---

## 4. Unlinking or Changing Telegram Account

If you change your phone number or wish to disconnect Telegram:
1. Return to **Profile → Notifications & Integrations**.
2. Click the red **Unlink Telegram Account** button.
3. Confirm the dialog. All future notifications will revert to SMS or in-app push.

# Siren / Bell Management

Configure school bell schedules and siren management with hardware webhook support. YeneSchool integrates with hardware systems for automated bell scheduling.

## Creating Bell Schedules

1. Navigate to **Siren Management** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Schedule**
3. Set the details:
   - **Schedule Name** — e.g., "Regular Schedule"
   - **Days** — Monday through Friday
   - **Period Times** — Link to period times from the timetable
   - **Bell Times** — When bells should ring
4. Click **Save**

## Hardware Webhooks

YeneSchool supports hardware webhooks for physical bell systems:

1. Navigate to **Siren Management → Webhooks**
2. Add webhook URLs for your hardware system
3. Test the connection
4. Click **Save**

## Period Time Integration

Bell schedules are automatically linked to period times from the timetable:

- When a period starts, the bell rings
- When a period ends, the bell rings
- Break periods have separate bell configurations

> [NOTE]
> Bell schedules are per-branch. Each branch can have its own bell schedule and hardware webhook configuration.

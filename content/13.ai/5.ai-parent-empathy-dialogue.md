# Autonomous AI Parent Dialogue & Empathetic Engagement

Parental involvement is one of the strongest predictors of student academic success. However, automated school messages often sound cold, robotic, or alarming. The **YeneSchool Autonomous Parent Dialogue Engine** (`ai-parent-dialogue.service.ts` & `ai-academic-outreach.service.ts`) provides warm, empathetic, multilingual communication that keeps parents informed, relieves anxiety, and builds trust between families and the school.

---

## 1. Empathetic Communication Principles

When discussing student grades, attendance dips, or behavioral notes, the AI operates under strict pedagogical and psychological guidelines:

```
[Warm Cultural Greeting] ──> [Affirm Student Strengths] ──> [Constructive Opportunity] ──> [Actionable At-Home Tips]
```

| Traditional Cold Message (Avoided) | YeneSchool Empathetic AI Approach (Delivered) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"Your son Abel scored 42% on Physics quiz. Grade is failing."* | *"Greetings Ato Dawit! We wanted to share an update on Abel's learning in Physics. While today's motion quiz was challenging (42%), Abel has shown great curiosity in class experiments. With 15 minutes of guided formula practice tonight, he will easily master this unit. Would you like a few practice questions to try together at home?"* |
| *"Student was absent today without excuse."* | *"Good morning Woizero Tigist! We missed seeing Sara in homeroom today. We hope everything is well with your family! If Sara is unwell or taking leave, simply reply to this message and we will excuse her attendance record for today."* |

## 2. Natural Language Sick Leave & Absence Recognition

Parents do not need to fill out complex administrative forms. When a parent texts the school bot or portal in **Amharic, Afaan Oromo, or English**, the autonomous AI naturally detects the intent:

### Real-World Examples Handled Automatically:
- **Amharic Input:** `"ልጄ ዮናስ ታሟል ነገ ትምህርት ቤት አይመጣም ፈቃድ ስጡት"`
- **English Input:** `"Good morning, my daughter Helen has a doctor's appointment tomorrow morning and will miss first period."`

### What the AI Automatically Executes:
1. Validates the parent's verified identity and child enrollment.
2. Replies with warm empathy: *"Thank you for letting us know! We wish Yonas a speedy recovery. We have recorded his excused medical absence for tomorrow and notified his homeroom teacher."*
3. Automatically updates the school attendance register with an **Excused Medical Leave** status so the student is not flagged for unexcused absence.
4. Generates an alert on the homeroom teacher's morning roll-call screen with the parent's note.

---

## 3. Intelligent Teacher Consultation Routing

When a parent expresses concern, emotional distress, or explicitly asks to speak with a human educator:
- **Detected Cues:** *"I want to talk with the teacher"*, *"መምህሩ ጋር አገናኙኝ"*, *"Can someone call me about his math grades?"*
- **Autonomous Action:**
  1. The AI acknowledges the request warmly: *"I will connect you directly with Abel's homeroom teacher, Teacher Aster."*
  2. Generates a **Parent Consultation Request** in Teacher Aster's inbox summarizing the parent's inquiry.
  3. Suggests available teacher office hours and sends a calendar invite to both parties.

---

## 4. Multilingual Cultural Support

The Parent Dialogue Engine seamlessly communicates across Ethiopia's primary languages:
- **Amharic (አማርኛ)**
- **Afaan Oromo (Afaan Oromoo)**
- **Somali (Soomaali)**
- **Tigrinya (ትግርኛ)**
- **Arabic (العربية)**
- **English**

Parents receive notifications and can reply in whichever language they feel most comfortable writing.

# Autonomous AI Engine & Background Autopilots

Beyond interactive chatbots, YeneSchool features an **Autonomous AI Agent Engine**. Operating silently in the background, these autonomous workers analyze school databases, detect operational bottlenecks, forecast student dropouts, draft bilingual announcements, and proactively alert directors and teachers before issues escalate.

---

## 1. The Autonomous AI Agent Suite

```
                               ┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
                               │     YeneSchool Autonomous AI Core       │
                               └────────────────────┬────────────────────┘
                                                    │
            ┌───────────────────┬───────────────────┼───────────────────┬───────────────────┐
            ▼                   ▼                   ▼                   ▼                   ▼
    [Director Morning]   [Standing Tasks]   [Early Warning]     [Auto-Outreach]    [Remedial Tutor]
      Briefing Agent      Background Worker   Risk Predictor      Parent Drafter     Adaptive Learning
```

### 1. Director Morning Briefing Autopilot (`ai-director-cron.service`)
Every morning at 07:30 AM (local time), the briefing autopilot analyzes yesterday's operations and today's schedule to deliver a concise executive bulleted summary to the director's dashboard and Telegram bot:
- **Attendance Anomalies:** Teachers who failed to submit period registers or classes with attendance drops >15%.
- **Fee Milestones:** Daily collections vs monthly targets and pending high-value balance payments.
- **Facility & Schedule Alerts:** Scheduled exam papers, siren schedule changes, and staff on leave.

### 2. Autonomous Standing Tasks (`ai-standing-task.service`)
School directors can assign natural language background monitoring tasks that execute periodically:
- *Example 1:* *"Monitor Grade 9 attendance every Friday. If any student has accumulated 3 or more unexcused absences this month, alert me and draft an SMS notice for the parents."*
- *Example 2:* *"Every Monday at 8:00 AM, send me a list of all teachers who have not submitted their weekly lesson plans for supervisor review."*

### 3. Student Early Warning Risk Engine (`ai-academic-outreach.service`)
The AI risk engine synthesizes academic grades, daily attendance trends, discipline logs, and fee payment history:
- Computes a dynamic composite **Risk Score (0–100)** for every student.
- Categorizes students into **Low Risk (Green)**, **Moderate Risk (Yellow)**, and **Critical Risk (Red)**.
- Recommends specific pedagogical interventions (e.g., *Schedule remedial algebra tutoring*, *Request parent conference*).

### 4. Bilingual Announcement Drafter (`ai-announcement-drafter.service`)
Drafting official school communications requires formal, culturally accurate bilingual formatting. The AI Announcement Drafter automatically converts brief bullet points into elegant **Amharic and English** notices:
- Formats official headers, greeting, body, and signature blocks.
- Verifies Ethiopian Calendar (E.C.) dates match corresponding Gregorian (G.C.) dates accurately.
- Automatically dispatches notices to the school Telegram Channel, parent SMS, and mobile push notifications upon director approval.

### 5. Adaptive Remedial Practice Tutor (`ai-remedial-practice.service`)
When a student completes an online test or quiz, the remedial AI reviews missed questions:
- Diagnoses the underlying conceptual misunderstanding.
- Generates 3–5 tailored practice questions with step-by-step explanations suited to the student's learning level.
- Tracks mastery improvement over time.

## 2. Configuring Autonomous AI Parameters

School Directors and School Directors can customize AI agent behavior in **Platform Settings → AI Configuration**:

| Setting | Options | Recommended Default | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Active AI LLM Model** | Gemini 1.5 Pro, Gemini 1.5 Flash, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, GPT-4o | `Gemini 1.5 Pro` | Primary reasoning engine for complex analytics and translation. |
| **Morning Briefing Time**| Time Picker (06:00 – 09:00) | `07:30 EAT` | Time daily executive briefing is delivered. |
| **Briefing Channels** | In-App Dashboard, Telegram Bot, Email | `Dashboard + Telegram` | Delivery destinations for director alerts. |
| **Risk Detection Threshold** | Sensitivity: Low, Medium, High | `Medium` | Sensitivity of attendance and academic flags. |
| **Auto-Draft Parent Notices**| Enabled / Disabled | `Enabled` | Pre-drafts outreach messages for director review. |

> [!NOTE]
> All automated AI actions (such as sending messages to parents or penalizing attendance) require human-in-the-loop authorization before final dispatch. The AI drafts and recommends; staff approve and send.

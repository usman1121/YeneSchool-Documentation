# Subjects & Lessons

Subjects organize the curriculum across your school. Teachers use lessons to plan and deliver content to their assigned classes.

## Creating Subjects

1. Navigate to **Subjects** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Subject**
3. Set the subject name (e.g., "Mathematics", "Amharic", "Science")
4. Select the subject category (e.g., Core, Elective, Language)
5. Click **Save**

## Lesson Plans

Teachers create lesson plans for each class they teach:

1. Go to **Lessons** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Lesson**
3. Select the class, section, and subject
4. Set the lesson title, description, and objectives
5. Add any materials or resources
6. Click **Save**

## Lesson Workflow

| Stage | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Draft** | Lesson is being prepared |
| **Published** | Lesson is available to students |
| **Completed** | Lesson is finished and graded |

## Subject Assignments

Subjects are assigned to classes through the **Class Management** page. Each subject assignment links a teacher to a class section.

> [!NOTE]
> Lessons appear on the **Teacher Dashboard** under **Quick Shortcuts → Lesson Plans**. Students can access published lessons from their dashboard.

# Academic Years & Terms

Academic years and terms are the foundation of your school's academic schedule in YeneSchool. Every student enrollment, attendance record, and grade is scoped to an academic year and term.

## Creating Academic Years

1. From the **Director Dashboard**, navigate to **Academic Years** in the sidebar
2. Click **Create Academic Year**
3. Fill in the details:
   - **Year Name** — e.g., "2025–2026"
   - **Start Date** — First day of the academic year
   - **End Date** — Last day of the academic year
   - **Calendar Type** — Ethiopian Calendar (EC) or Gregorian Calendar (GC)
4. Click **Save**

## Adding Terms

Each academic year can have multiple terms:

1. Open an academic year from the list
2. Click **Add Term**
3. Set the term name (e.g., "First Term", "Second Term", "Third Term")
4. Set the start and end dates
5. Click **Save**

## Term Status Workflow

| Status | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Active** | Current term — attendance and grades are recorded |
| **Upcoming** | Not started yet — schedules can be created |
| **Closed** | Ended — data becomes read-only for most users |

> [!TIP]
> Only one term can be **Active** at a time. Switch terms from the **Settings → School Settings** page.

## Calendar Support

YeneSchool supports both the **Ethiopian Calendar (EC)** and **Gregorian Calendar (GC)**. Your school can choose which calendar to use for each academic year.

> [!NOTE]
> The calendar type is set per academic year. You can have some years on EC and others on GC.

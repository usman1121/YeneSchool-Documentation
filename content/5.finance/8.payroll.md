# Payroll

The Finance module includes payroll management for staff salaries. Create salary structures, record payments, and generate payroll reports.

## Creating Salary Structures

1. Navigate to **Finance → Payroll**
2. Click **Create Salary Structure**
3. Set the details:
   - **Position** — e.g., Teacher, Director, Registrar
   - **Base Salary** — Monthly base amount
   - **Allowances** — Housing, transport, etc.
   - **Deductions** — Insurance, tax, etc.
   - **Net Salary** — Automatically calculated
4. Click **Save**

## Recording Payments

1. Open a staff member's payroll
2. Click **Record Payment**
3. Set the payment month, amount, and method
4. Click **Save**

## Payroll Reports

Generate payroll reports filtered by:
- **Month** — Specific payment period
- **Position** — Filter by job role
- **Branch** — Filter by branch
- **Status** — Paid, Pending, Overdue

> [!NOTE]
> Payroll data is scoped to the staff member's assigned branch. Branch directors can only see payroll for their branch.

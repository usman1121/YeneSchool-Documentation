# Installment Plans

When you assign a fee structure to students, YeneSchool automatically generates an **installment plan** — a set of individual payment milestones with amounts and due dates. Understanding how installments work helps you track what is due and when.

## How Installments Are Generated

Installment plans are created automatically based on the **Billing Method** of the linked fee structure:

| Billing Method | Number of Installments | Due Date Logic |
|---------------|----------------------|----------------|
| Full Payment | 1 | Start of academic year |
| Per Term | 1 per term | Start of each term |
| Per Semester | 1 per semester | Start of each semester |
| Monthly | 10 | 1st of each Ethiopian school month |

> [!NOTE]
> Due dates are calculated using the **Ethiopian Calendar (EC)**. The first installment falls on the first day of the school year, and subsequent ones follow month-by-month or term-by-term.

## Assigning a Fee Structure to a Student

1. Navigate to the **Outstanding Balances** tab on the Finance dashboard.
2. Search for the student by name or student code.
3. If no fee record exists yet, go to **Students → Student Profile** and assign a fee structure from their finance section.
4. Once assigned, the system generates all installments immediately and they appear in the student's fee balance.

## Viewing a Student's Installment Schedule

1. Click **Record Payment** on the Finance dashboard.
2. Search for the student's name in the search box.
3. Select the student from the results.
4. Open the **Fee Item** dropdown — each installment (e.g., *Tuition – Meskerem*, *Tuition – Tikimt*) is listed separately with its remaining balance.

> [!TIP]
> When a student has multiple fee items, always confirm which installment you are recording the payment against before saving — each installment is tracked separately.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Partial payments are supported. You do not need to pay the full installment amount at once; the remaining balance stays on record until fully settled.

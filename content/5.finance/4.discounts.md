# Discount Policies

YeneSchool lets you define **Discount Policies** at the school level and then apply them to individual students. Discounts reduce the effective balance a student owes on their fee items.

## Types of Discounts

| Type | How It Works | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Percentage** | Reduces the fee by a percentage of the total | 25% sibling discount |
| **Fixed Amount** | Reduces the fee by a set ETB amount | ETB 500 off registration |

## Creating a Discount Policy

Discount policies are managed by your school administrator. If you need a new policy (e.g., a new scholarship tier), ask your Admin or Super Admin to create it under **Settings → Discount Policies**.

The policy fields are:
- **Name** — e.g., *Sibling Discount*, *Merit Scholarship*, *Staff Child*.
- **Type** — Percentage or Fixed Amount.
- **Value** — the percentage number or the fixed ETB amount.

## Applying a Discount to a Student

1. Go to **Students** in the sidebar and open the student's profile.
2. Navigate to the **Finance** tab on the student's profile page.
3. Click **Apply Discount**.
4. Select the applicable **Discount Policy** from the dropdown.
5. Confirm the discount value shown is correct.
6. Click **Save**. The student's outstanding balance updates immediately.

> [!TIP]
> For sibling discounts, apply the policy to the younger sibling's profile. The reduced balance shows on the Outstanding Balances tab automatically.

> [!WARNING]
> Discounts are applied to the **total fee**, not to individual installments. Once applied, the remaining balance across all unpaid installments is recalculated proportionally.

> [!NOTE]
> All applied discounts are visible in the **Total Discounts** summary card on the Finance dashboard and are recorded in the Finance Audit Log for accountability.

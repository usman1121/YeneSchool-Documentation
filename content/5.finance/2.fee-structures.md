# Fee Structures

A **Fee Structure** defines how much students are charged and how often. You can create separate fee structures for different grade levels, fee types (tuition, exam, library, etc.), and academic years.

## Creating a Fee Structure

1. From the Finance dashboard, click **Add Fee Structure** in the top-right corner.
2. Select the **Fee Type** from the dropdown — for example, *Tuition*, *Registration*, *Exam Fee*, or *Library*.
3. Enter the **Amount** in ETB.
4. Choose a **Billing Mode**:
   - **Single** — one fixed charge for the whole year.
   - **Per Term / Per Semester / Per Quarter** — split across school periods.
   - **Monthly** — 10 equal installments (one per Ethiopian school month).
5. Set the **Grade Scope**:
   - **All Grades** — applies to every student.
   - **Single Grade** — pick one grade level.
   - **Grade Range** — choose a *From* and *To* grade.
6. If your school uses terms, select the **Term** the fee applies to (or leave blank for year-wide fees).
7. Optionally add a **Description** for internal reference.
8. Click **Create Fee Structure** to save.

> [!NOTE]
> The Ethiopian school year has **10 months**, not 12. A Monthly billing structure therefore generates **10 installments** automatically — not 12.

> [!TIP]
> Create separate fee structures for each grade band (e.g., Grade 1–4, Grade 5–8, Grade 9–12) if tuition rates differ across levels.

## Billing Methods at a Glance

| Billing Method | Installments Generated | Typical Use |
|---------------|----------------------|-------------|
| Full Payment | 1 | Registration fees |
| Per Term | 1 per term | Termly tuition |
| Monthly | 10 | Monthly tuition |
| Per Semester | 1 per semester | Semester schools |

> [!WARNING]
> Once a fee structure has payments recorded against it, you cannot delete it. Contact your system administrator if a correction is needed.

# Recording a Payment

Use this workflow whenever a student or their parent pays a school fee. After saving, YeneSchool automatically generates a numbered receipt and updates the student's outstanding balance.

## Steps to Record a Payment

1. On the Finance dashboard, click the **Record Payment** button in the top-right corner. The payment dialog opens.
2. In the **Search Student** field, type the student's name or student code. Wait for the search results to appear below the field.
3. Click the correct student from the results list. Their name and class are shown to help you confirm.
4. In the **Fee Item** dropdown, select the specific fee installment you are recording payment for — for example, *Tuition – Hidar* or *Exam Fee – Term 1*. The remaining balance for that item is shown alongside each option.
5. Enter the **Amount Paid** in ETB. This can be less than the full balance (partial payment).
6. Select the **Payment Method**:
   - **Cash** — student paid in person with cash.
   - **Bank Transfer** — payment made via bank deposit.
   - **Cheque** — payment by cheque.
7. If paying by bank transfer or cheque, enter the **Transaction Reference** number (e.g., bank slip number or cheque number).
8. Optionally add **Notes** for internal reference (e.g., *Parent paid for both terms at once*).
9. Click **Record Payment** to save. A success toast confirms the payment and the receipt number.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Double-check the student name and fee item before saving. Payments are recorded against the Finance Audit Log and require an administrator to reverse.

> [!TIP]
> To print the receipt immediately after recording, click **View Receipt** in the success notification, then use your browser's print function or the **Download PDF** button.

## Payment Methods Summary

| Method | When to Use | Reference Field |
|--------|-------------|----------------|
| Cash | In-person cash collection | Not required |
| Bank Transfer | CBE/Awash/Abyssinia deposit | Bank slip number |
| Cheque | Company or parent cheque | Cheque number |

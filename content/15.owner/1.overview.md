# School Owner Executive Overview

The **School Owner Portal** provides proprietors, board members, and executive leaders with high-level operational oversight, financial health indicators, and strategic analytics across all school campuses and branches.

---

## 1. Executive KPI Dashboard

The Owner Dashboard aggregates data across the entire institution into actionable executive metrics:

| Executive Metric | What It Measures | Strategic Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Total Enrolled Students** | Total active student body across all branches and grade levels. | Monitor enrollment growth and campus capacity utilization. |
| **Net Fee Collection Rate** | Percentage of billed tuition and transport revenue collected to date. | Immediate visibility into cash flow and working capital health. |
| **Outstanding Receivables** | Total uncollected tuition, transport, and ancillary fees. | Identify revenue leakage and prioritize debt collection efforts. |
| **Staff Retention Rate** | Percentage of teaching and administrative staff retained year-over-year. | Track employee satisfaction and instructional stability. |
| **School Health Index** | Composite score (0–100) factoring attendance, pass rates, fee health, and compliance. | Quick pulse check on overall institutional performance. |

---

## 2. Strategic Modules

1. **Multi-Branch Analytics:** Compare enrollment, revenue, and academic standards across separate campuses in real time.
2. **Financial Forecasting:** Project future term revenue based on fee schedules and historical collection trends.
3. **Staff Compensation & Payroll:** Review total wage liabilities, allowances, and tax withholding summaries.
4. **Director Performance:** Monitor director KPI fulfillment, attendance audit compliance, and parent satisfaction scores.

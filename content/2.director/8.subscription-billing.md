# School Subscription & Billing Management

The **Subscription & Billing Center** (`/director/subscription`) enables School Directors and Proprietors to monitor institutional plan usage, review active feature modules, calculate upgrade costs, process renewals, and download official value-added tax (VAT) invoices.

## 2. Monitoring Real-Time Plan Utilization

In **Director → Subscription**, the top KPI overview tracks key institutional quotas:

- **Active Student Seats:** Real-time enrolled student count vs maximum tier capacity (e.g., *742 / 1,000 Enrolled*). Color turns amber at 90% utilization.
- **Billing Cycle & Expiry Date:** Displays remaining days in the active semester or annual subscription term.
- **AI Token Balance:** Monthly tokens consumed across lesson planning, risk prediction, and automated parent translations.

---

## 3. Upgrading Your Plan or Adding Student Capacity

When your school enrollment expands:

1. Click **Upgrade Subscription Plan** in the top-right header.
2. Select your desired new tier (*Professional* or *Enterprise*) and choose your billing cycle:
   - **Per Semester:** Billed bi-annually at term commencement.
   - **Annual Plan (Recommended):** Includes a **15% discount** and priority onboarding support.
3. Review the calculated total in **Ethiopian Birr (ETB)**.

---

## 4. Payment Methods & Verification

YeneSchool supports direct Ethiopian digital payment gateways and traditional bank transfers:

### Option 1: Telebirr & CBE Birr (Instant Automated Activation)
1. Select **Telebirr** or **CBE Birr** in the payment modal.
2. Enter your account phone number and authorize the push USSD prompt, or scan the dynamic merchant QR code.
3. Once authorized, the transaction verifies within 15 seconds, and your new student limit activates automatically.

### Option 2: Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE) / Bank Wire Transfer
1. Select **Direct Bank Transfer**.
2. Transfer the invoice amount to the official YeneSchool institutional account:
   - **Bank:** Commercial Bank of Ethiopia (CBE)
   - **Account Name:** YeneSchool Technologies PLC
   - **Account Number:** `1000-XXXXXXXXXX`
3. In the verification form:
   - Enter the **Bank Transaction / Reference ID** (FT Number).
   - Upload a photo or PDF scan of the bank deposit slip.
4. Click **Submit Payment for Verification**. The finance team reviews and verifies transfers within 2 business hours.

---

## 5. Invoices & Payment Receipts

- Every processed renewal or tier upgrade generates a downloadable **Official Electronic VAT Receipt & Tax Invoice** stamped with the YeneSchool corporate seal.
- Access previous receipts at any time under **Billing History → Download PDF**.

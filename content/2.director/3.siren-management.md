# Siren & School Bell Management

YeneSchool includes an integrated **Siren & Automated Bell System** that can control both in-app audible chimes and physical bell relays via IoT webhook controllers.

---

## Configuring Period Bell Schedules

1. Go to **Director** → **Siren Management**.
2. Select the active **Period Scheme** (e.g., *Regular Monday–Friday Schedule*, *Friday Half-Day Schedule*, or *Exam Period Scheme*).
3. Click **Add Bell Event** or edit an existing period slot:
   - **Time**: e.g., `08:00 AM`, `08:45 AM`, `10:15 AM (Break Start)`, `10:30 AM (Break End)`
   - **Sound Type**: Single Ring, Double Chime, Long Siren, or Custom Audio File
   - **Target Zones**: All Campuses, Main Campus Only, or Specific Building Block
4. Toggle **Active** and click **Save Schedule**.

---

## Emergency Alarms & Manual Trigger

In addition to scheduled daily bells, authorized Directors and Administrators can trigger instant signals:

- **Emergency Siren**: High-priority alert broadcast across all connected speakers and staff dashboards.
- **Fire Drill Mode**: Timed evacuation countdown pattern.
- **Instant Bell**: Single-click manual chime for ad-hoc assembly calls.

> [!WARNING]
> Emergency alarms sound immediately across all campus audio endpoints and display a prominent red screen overlay on all active staff devices.

---

## Hardware Relay Integration

YeneSchool supports ESP32 and Raspberry Pi based relay boards connected to physical bells.

1. Under **Siren Settings**, locate the **Webhook Endpoint URL** and **Device Secret Key**.
2. Flash your local IoT relay controller with the YeneSchool Bell Client firmware.
3. Verify connection via the **Test Hardware Ping** button in the dashboard.

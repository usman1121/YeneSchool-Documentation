# School-Wide Settings & Configuration

The **School Settings Console** (`/settings/school`) is the administrative control center where the School Director defines institutional rules, academic calendar defaults, grading thresholds, attendance policies, and official branding.

---

## 1. Institutional Identity & Official Branding

Configure the official identity used across printed report cards, transcripts, student ID cards, and receipts:

| Setting Field | Description | Example / Standard Format |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **School Legal Name** | Official registered name recognized by the Ministry of Education. | *Finfine International Academy* |
| **School Motto / Slogan** | Displayed in report card footers and login portal. | *"Excellence in Knowledge & Character"* |
| **Ministry Registration #** | Accreditation registration code. | *MOE/AA/2012/8492* |
| **School Logo (Light & Dark)**| High-resolution PNG/SVG crest. | 512x512 transparent PNG. |
| **Official Stamp / Seal** | Digitalized director seal stamped on PDF report cards. | High-contrast circular PNG stamp. |
| **Contact Address & Phone** | Physical campus address, P.O. Box, official phone, and email. | *Bole Sub-City, Woreda 03, Addis Ababa* |

---

## 2. Academic Calendar & Term Configuration

1. In **Settings → Academic Configuration**:
   - **Primary Calendar System:** Choose between **Ethiopian Calendar (E.C.)** (*Meskerem to Pagume*) or **Gregorian Calendar (G.C.)** (*September to June*).
   - **Academic Term Framework:** Select **Semester System** (2 Terms per year) or **Quarter System** (4 Quarters per year).
   - **Weekly Operating Days:** Select operating school days (e.g., *Monday through Friday* or *Monday through Saturday for half-day tutorials*).
   - **Daily Periods Count:** Set default class periods per day (e.g., *7 Periods of 45 minutes each*).

---

## 3. Grading Scales & Passing Standards

Define the grading formula applied across all subjects and terminal report cards:

```
Score: 90–100% ──> Grade A (4.0) | "Excellent"
Score: 80–89%  ──> Grade B (3.0) | "Very Good"
Score: 70–79%  ──> Grade C (2.0) | "Satisfactory"
Score: 60–69%  ──> Grade D (1.0) | "Passing"
Score:  0–59%  ──> Grade F (0.0) | "Fail / Remedial Required"
```

- **Pass Mark Cutoff:** Minimum overall cumulative average required for year-end promotion (standard is *50%* or *60%*).
- **Core Subject Requirement:** Option to mandate passing marks in core national subjects (e.g., *Mathematics and English must be >= 50% regardless of overall GPA*).

---

## 4. Attendance Cutoffs & Policy Thresholds

1. **Morning Gate Cutoff Time:** Time after which arriving students are marked *Late* (e.g., *08:15 AM*).
2. **Absence Penalty Warning Threshold:** Cumulative unexcused absences that trigger automated parent SMS alerts (e.g., *3 Absences in a Month*).
3. **Disciplinary Threshold:** Number of late arrivals converted into one unexcused absence (e.g., *3 Lates = 1 Absence*).

---

## 5. Financial & Billing Settings

- **Default Currency:** `ETB (Ethiopian Birr)`.
- **Payment Grace Period:** Number of calendar days after the 1st of the month before late fees apply (e.g., *10 Days*).
- **Accepted Payment Methods:** Toggle Cash, Bank Transfer (CBE, Awash, Dashen, Abyssinia), Telebirr, and CBE Birr.
- **School Bank Account Numbers:** Configured bank details printed automatically on student payment voucher invoices.

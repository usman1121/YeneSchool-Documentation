# Reports, Analytics & Data Health

YeneSchool provides a centralized reporting suite that aggregates operational, academic, attendance, and financial data into exportable tables and interactive visual charts.

---

## Types of Available Reports

| Report Category | Description | Export Formats |
|-----------------|-------------|----------------|
| **Attendance Summaries** | Daily, weekly, monthly, and per-term attendance rates by class, gender, and student. | PDF, Excel (XLSX), CSV |
| **Academic Performance** | Grade distribution curves, subject pass/fail ratios, and ranking leaderboards. | PDF, Excel (XLSX) |
| **Discipline Logs** | Breakdown of behavioral incidents, action taken, and recurring infractions. | PDF, CSV |
| **Staff Workload** | Teaching period allocations, subject coverage, and teacher attendance. | PDF, Excel (XLSX) |
| **Financial Health** | Fee collection rates, outstanding balances by grade, and revenue forecasts. | Excel (XLSX), CSV |

---

## Running Data Consistency & Health Checks

To prevent data issues (such as unassigned students, duplicate enrollments, or missing grades prior to report card generation):

1. Navigate to **Director** → **Reports** → **Data Quality**.
2. Click **Run Health Diagnostic**.
3. The system scans for common anomalies:
   - Students without assigned sections
   - Subjects missing assigned teachers
   - Incomplete grade entries before end-of-term deadlines
   - Unbalanced timetable period allocations
4. Click **Resolve** next to any detected issue to jump straight to the fix screen.

> [!TIP]
> Always run the **Data Quality Diagnostic** one week before generating end-of-term report cards to catch any missing grades early.

# Inventory & Store Management Overview

The **YeneSchool Inventory & Store Management Module** enables schools to track tangible assets, manage supply room consumables, monitor textbook distributions, handle uniform sales, and process departmental requisitions efficiently.

---

## 1. Key Inventory Capabilities

- **Catalog & Categorization:** Organize inventory by categories such as Textbooks, Science Lab Supplies, Sports Equipment, Office Stationery, School Uniforms, and ICT Assets.
- **Stock Movements:** Record Stock In (Purchases/Donations), Stock Out (Issuances/Disposals), and Inter-branch Transfers with complete audit logging.
- **Requisition Workflow:** Teachers and department heads submit digital requests for classroom supplies, reviewed and approved by administrators or storekeepers.
- **Low-Stock Early Warning:** Automated threshold notifications when consumable levels fall below safety minimums.
- **School Store Sales:** Process student uniform purchases, exercise books, and badge sales directly through the platform.

---

## 2. Storekeeper Dashboard Summary

| KPI Card | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Total Inventory Value** | Cumulative monetary valuation of all items in stock based on purchase cost. |
| **Active SKUs / Items** | Count of distinct catalog item types currently tracked in the system. |
| **Low Stock Warnings** | Number of items currently at or below their designated reorder threshold. |
| **Pending Requisitions**| Supply requests submitted by teachers and staff awaiting approval or fulfillment. |

> [!TIP]
> Perform a physical stock audit at the end of each academic term using the **Inventory Reconciliation Report** to identify discrepancies between physical counts and system balances.

# Stock In, Stock Out & Requisitions

Track every movement of supplies and assets into and out of the school storerooms with digital receipts and audit logs.

---

## 1. Recording Stock In (Purchases & Deliveries)

When new shipments or purchases arrive at the school:

1. Navigate to **Inventory → Transactions → Stock In**.
2. Click **+ New Stock In Entry**.
3. Enter:
   - **Supplier / Vendor Name:** (e.g., *Birhan Stationery Wholesalers*).
   - **Invoice / Delivery Note #:** Vendor invoice reference.
   - **Received Date:** Date goods were accepted.
   - **Items & Quantities:** Select items, specify quantities received, and enter unit purchase prices.
4. Upload a photo or PDF scan of the signed delivery receipt.
5. Click **Confirm & Update Stock**. Current on-hand balances increment automatically.

---

## 2. Teacher Requisition Workflow

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A["📝 Teacher Submits Request"] --> B["👁️ Storekeeper / Director Reviews"]
    B --> C["📦 Items Issued & Stock Deducted"]
```

1. **Submission:** Teachers navigate to **Store Requisitions** and choose items required for their classroom (e.g., *Whiteboard Markers, Chalk, Graph Paper*).
2. **Review:** The storekeeper or director receives a notification, inspects on-hand stock, and clicks **Approve** or **Decline with Reason**.
3. **Fulfillment:** When the teacher collects the supplies, the storekeeper clicks **Mark Issued**, automatically reducing inventory levels and logging the transaction to the teacher's profile.

---

## 3. Damaged Goods & Write-Offs

When items become damaged, expired, or lost:
1. Go to **Inventory → Write-Offs**.
2. Select the item, quantity to remove, and reason (*Damaged, Expired, Lost during transport*).
3. Require director authorization before permanent deduction from balance sheets.

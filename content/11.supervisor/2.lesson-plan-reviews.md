# Lesson Plan Approvals & Feedback

Supervisors review and approve lesson plans to ensure teachers align daily classroom instruction with national curriculum standards and school pedagogical goals.

---

## 1. Accessing the Review Queue

1. Navigate to **Supervisor → Lesson Plans**.
2. Filter the queue by **Department / Subject**, **Grade Level**, or **Status** (*Pending Review, Approved, Needs Revision*).
3. Click on a teacher's weekly plan to inspect:
   - **Unit & Topic:** Subject area and lesson chapter.
   - **Learning Objectives:** Measurable student learning outcomes (Bloom's Taxonomy).
   - **Teacher Activities & Student Tasks:** Time allocation across Introduction, Direct Instruction, Guided Practice, and Independent Work.
   - **Assessment & Homework:** Quizzes, homework questions, and rubrics.
   - **Attached Resources:** Slide decks, worksheets, or lab manuals.

---

## 2. Taking Action on a Plan

| Action | When to Use | Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Approve** | Plan meets all curricular standards, clear objectives, and varied pedagogical activities. | Teacher receives approval notification; lesson is tagged as verified. |
| **Request Revision** | Plan lacks clear learning outcomes, missing assessment tasks, or timing is unrealistic. | Opens a feedback dialog. The plan returns to the teacher's edit queue with specific notes. |
| **Add Comment** | Provide pedagogical suggestions or share supplementary materials without rejecting the plan. | Adds inline notes visible on the teacher's lesson editor. |

> [!TIP]
> Encourage teachers to submit lesson plans by Friday afternoon so supervisors have adequate time to review before Monday morning classes commence.

# Academic Supervision Overview

The **Academic Supervisor** role is designed for department heads, subject coordinators, and pedagogical inspectors. Supervisors maintain high educational standards by reviewing lesson plans, conducting classroom observations, tracking syllabus pacing, and mentoring teaching staff.

---

## 1. Supervisor Dashboard Highlights

| Widget | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Lesson Plan Review Queue** | Weekly lesson plans submitted by teachers pending supervisor sign-off. |
| **Classroom Observations** | Scheduled observation visits, rubric scoring sheets, and teacher feedback reports. |
| **Curriculum Pacing Gauge** | Visual percentage of planned curriculum units completed vs expected academic calendar milestone. |
| **Department Performance** | Subject grade distributions, test averages, and student pass rates across classes. |

---

## 2. Supervision Workflow

1. **Weekly Lesson Plan Approvals:** Review pedagogical objectives, instructional methods, and assessment tasks before teaching begins.
2. **Classroom Visits:** Evaluate teacher pedagogical delivery against standardized rubric criteria.
3. **Targeted Mentoring:** Provide actionable constructive feedback and assign development action items.
4. **Director Reporting:** Generate department-wide academic health summaries for the school director.

# Classroom Observations & Rubrics

Conduct structured in-class observations using digital rubrics to assess teacher effectiveness, student engagement, and classroom management.

---

## 1. Scheduling an Observation

1. Go to **Supervisor → Observations**.
2. Click **+ Schedule Observation**.
3. Select the **Teacher**, **Subject / Period**, **Classroom Section**, and **Date**.
4. Choose the evaluation type: *Formal Term Observation, Peer Review, or New Teacher Induction Check*.
5. Notify the teacher automatically via email and in-app alert.

---

## 2. Standard Observation Rubric Dimensions

During the observation, rate the teacher across standard 4-point scales (*1 = Unsatisfactory, 2 = Developing, 3 = Proficient, 4 = Exemplary*):

| Evaluation Domain | Key Observation Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Pedagogical Delivery** | Clear explanations, subject mastery, effective use of teaching aids/blackboard, check for understanding. |
| **2. Student Engagement** | Active student participation, inclusive questioning, group collaboration, equitable student involvement. |
| **3. Classroom Management** | Time management, smooth transitions between activities, clear behavior expectations, positive rapport. |
| **4. Assessment & Feedback**| Formative assessment during class, constructive feedback on student errors, clear homework instructions. |

---

## 3. Post-Observation Conference & Sign-Off

1. Calculate the overall weighted observation score.
2. Write **Key Strengths** and **Areas for Growth**.
3. Set 1–3 concrete **Action Goals** for the next observation cycle.
4. Schedule a 10-minute debrief conference with the teacher to discuss findings.
5. Both the supervisor and teacher digitally sign the completed evaluation record.

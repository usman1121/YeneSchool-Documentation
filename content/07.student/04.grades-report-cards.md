# Grades & Terminal Report Cards

YeneSchool provides complete academic transparency. Students can track continuous assessments in real-time throughout the semester and access official report cards at term end.

---

## 1. Real-Time Gradebook Breakdown

Navigate to **Grades** in the student menu to inspect your performance across all subjects. Each subject card reveals:

| Assessment Component | Weight (Typical) | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Quizzes & Tests** | 20% | Continuous in-class assessments and weekly quizzes. |
| **Assignments & Projects**| 15% | Homework, lab reports, and group research assignments. |
| **Midterm Examination** | 25% | Comprehensive mid-semester exam. |
| **Final Examination** | 40% | End-of-term standardized examination. |
| **Total Mark & Letter Grade** | 100% | Cumulative score with assigned letter grade (e.g., *A, B+, B, C*). |

---

## 2. Viewing and Downloading Report Cards

At the end of each semester or academic term, the school registrar issues certified digital report cards:

1. Click **Report Cards** in the sidebar.
2. Select the **Academic Year** (e.g., *2016 E.C. / 2023-2024 G.C.*) and **Term** (*Term 1, Term 2, or Final Year Summary*).
3. The report card preview displays:
   - Full subject list with component scores and final term marks.
   - Class average comparison and subject ranking.
   - Attendance percentage and conduct / discipline rating.
   - Homeroom teacher and school director signature stamps.
4. Click **Download PDF** to save an official, print-ready copy for your records.

> [!NOTE]
> If a report card is locked due to outstanding school fees or unreturned library books, contact the school Finance Office or Registrar to clear clearance holds.

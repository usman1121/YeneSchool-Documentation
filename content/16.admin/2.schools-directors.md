# Onboarding Schools & Directors

---

## 1. Creating a New School Instance

1. Log in to the **Super Admin Console** (`/superadmin`).
2. Navigate to **Schools → + Create School**.
3. Complete the institution setup form:
   - **School Legal Name:** (e.g., *Finfine International Academy*).
   - **Slug / Subdomain:** (e.g., *finfine* → accessible at `finfine.yeneschool.com`).
   - **Contact Email & Phone:** Primary administrative contact.
   - **Location / Region:** Addis Ababa, Oromia, Amhara, Sidama, Somali, etc.
   - **Default Calendar:** Ethiopian Calendar (EC) or Gregorian Calendar (GC).
   - **Subscription Tier:** Starter (up to 300 students), Professional (up to 1,500 students), or Enterprise (Unlimited).

---

## 2. Provisioning Director Credentials

1. In the school creation modal, fill in the **Initial School Director** details:
   - Full Name, Official Email Address, and Phone Number.
2. Click **Create School & Send Welcome Credentials**.
3. The platform generates a secure, randomized temporary password and delivers an onboarding email and SMS notification to the director with initial login instructions.

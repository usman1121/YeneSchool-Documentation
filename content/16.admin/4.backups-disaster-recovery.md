# Backups & Disaster Recovery

Data security and zero-data-loss resilience are core pillars of YeneSchool.

---

## 1. Automated Snapshot Schedule

- **Daily Full Snapshot:** Complete PostgreSQL database dump executed every night at 02:00 EAT (UTC+3).
- **WAL Archiving (Continuous):** Write-Ahead Logs streamed continuously to encrypted off-site cloud storage (AWS S3 / Cloudflare R2) enabling Point-In-Time Recovery (PITR) up to the minute.
- **Snapshot Retention Policy:** Daily backups retained for 30 days; weekly backups retained for 12 weeks; monthly backups retained for 7 years for regulatory compliance.

---

## 2. Manual Backup & Restore Procedures

To trigger an immediate backup before a major version upgrade:
1. Navigate to **Superadmin → Backups**.
2. Click **+ Trigger Immediate Backup**.
3. Choose scope: *Entire Platform Database* or *Specific School Tenant Snapshot*.
4. Download the encrypted `.sql.gz` dump or archive to remote storage.

> [!CAUTION]
> Restoring a database snapshot will overwrite existing records. Restoration requires Super Admin multi-factor authentication (MFA) and authorization confirmation.

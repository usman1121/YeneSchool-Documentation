# AI Quotas & Token Management

YeneSchool integrates advanced AI models (such as Gemini 1.5 Pro and Flash) to power lesson generation, student risk predictions, and intelligent question creation. Super Admins monitor and regulate token consumption across schools.

---

## 1. Setting School AI Quotas

1. Go to **Superadmin → AI Quotas & Usage**.
2. Select an institution to configure:
   - **Monthly Token Allowance:** (e.g., *5,000,000 Tokens/Month*).
   - **Allowed AI Features:** Toggle AI Lesson Planner, AI Quiz Generator, Student Risk Predictor, or Director Insights.
   - **Rate Limits:** Cap maximum requests per minute (RPM) to prevent accidental API exhaustion.

---

## 2. Usage Monitoring & Alerts

| Metric | Description | Recommended Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Consumed Tokens** | Cumulative tokens used by teachers, directors, and parents this billing cycle. | Review if usage approaches 85% of monthly allowance. |
| **Top AI Users** | List of teachers and staff generating the highest volume of AI prompts. | Identify champions for pedagogical best-practice sharing. |
| **Failed Requests** | AI generation requests that failed due to timeout or safety filters. | Inspect error telemetry to fine-tune system prompts. |

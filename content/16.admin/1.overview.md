# Super Admin Overview

The **Super Admin Console** is reserved for platform administrators who manage the multi-tenant infrastructure of YeneSchool, onboard new educational institutions, manage subscription tiers, and monitor system health.

---

## 1. Super Admin Core Responsibilities

```
[Onboard New School] ──> [Configure Quotas & Plan] ──> [Assign Director] ──> [Monitor Health & AI Usage]
```

- **Tenant Provisioning:** Create isolated school tenant environments with custom subdomains or custom domains.
- **Subscription & Licensing:** Manage feature access, student cap limits, and renewal dates across Free, Standard, and Enterprise tiers.
- **AI Token Quotas:** Allocate and monitor monthly Gemini/OpenAI token allowances for school AI assistants.
- **Data Integrity & Backups:** Schedule automated database snapshots and manage point-in-time disaster recovery.
- **System Telemetry:** Track server response times, active WebSocket connections, and error exception logs.

# Exam Seating & Hall Allocation

The **Exam Seating Engine** in YeneSchool simplifies exam logistics by generating randomized, conflict-free seating plans across examination halls and classrooms.

---

## 1. Setting Up Examination Halls

1. Navigate to **Director** → **Assessments** → **Exam Halls**.
2. Click **Create Hall / Room**:
   - **Room Name / Number**: e.g., *Main Hall A*, *Room 204*
   - **Total Capacity**: Number of desks available
   - **Grid Layout**: Specify rows and columns (e.g., 6 rows × 5 columns = 30 seats)
   - **Campus / Block**: Assign to a specific physical branch or building.

---

## 2. Generating Seating Arrangements

1. Go to **Assessments** → **Seating Generator**.
2. Select the **Exam Session** and the target **Classes** participating.
3. Choose the allocation strategy:
   - **Sequential by ID**: Order students alphabetically or by student ID.
   - **Randomized Shuffle**: Shuffle students across grades to ensure adjacent desks belong to different grades/subjects.
   - **Interleaved Classes**: Alternate between Grade 9 and Grade 10 students row-by-row.
4. Click **Generate Seating Plan**.

---

## 3. Printing Desk Slips & Hall Rosters

Once generated, you can export and print:

- **Desk Slips**: Small printable labels containing student name, photo, ID, and seat number to stick onto physical desks.
- **Hall Entry Rosters**: Master attendance sheets for invigilators to record student presence during the exam.
- **Student Exam Passes**: Printable admit cards distributed to students prior to exam week.

> [!TIP]
> Interleaved seating significantly reduces cheating opportunities by ensuring adjacent students are writing different subject papers.
